I'm a linguistics grad student at UMD[^1], working on formal semantics and syntax. My main research studies the relation between modal flavors and their syntactic properties (among other stuff). I work on modal ellipsis in that regard, since I believe it gives insight into this question. My approach is theoretical and experimental. I also have a strong interest in logic, philosophy, and psycho- and neurolinguistics, and I'm pursuing the NACS certificate alongside the PhD. I have a longstanding interest in literature too, mostly through listening to friends who chose that other path in studying language.

Given these interests, I'm advised by [Valentine Hacquard](https://linguistics.umd.edu/directory/valentine-hacquard) and [Alexander Williams](https://www.alexanderwilliams.org/home), and was previously advised in Geneva by [Isabelle Charnavel](https://www.unige.ch/lettres/linguistique/collaborateurs/collaborateurs-de-lenseignement-et-de-la-recherche/professeures-enseignantes/isabelle-charnavel) and [Anouk Dieuleveut](https://anoukdieuleveut.wordpress.com). I also benefit greatly from the help of friends and colleagues, including [Utku Türk](https://www.utkuturk.com/) and [David Müller](https://sites.google.com/view/davidmueller/startseite).
