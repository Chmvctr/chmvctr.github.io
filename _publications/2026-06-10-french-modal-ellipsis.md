This project in collaboration with [Anouk Dieuleveut](https://anoukdieuleveut.wordpress.com) and [David Müller](https://sites.google.com/view/davidmueller/startseite) investigates French modal ellipsis and anaphora, with particular attention to the availability of epistemic readings. It presents evidence from an acceptability-judgment experiment with 213 participants. The proceeding is coming, and certainly a follow-up experiment.
