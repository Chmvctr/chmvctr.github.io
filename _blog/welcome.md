Hello, and welcome to my site.

I'm Victor, a PhD student in Linguistics at the University of Maryland. I work on modality, the way we talk about possibilities and necessities, and more broadly on linguistic displacement: we can describe states of affairs other than the one we're in. More precisely, I'm interested in how the syntactic behavior of modal expressions shifts systematically depending on their flavor, or the opposite: how, for example, the structures modal (semi-)auxiliaries appear in seem to fix their interpretation.

This connects to a broader interest in the computational architecture of language, or anything cognition related (like what are the representations in our brain, what are the levels, how do we learn, etc.). I think empirical puzzles in theoretical linguistics, approached formally and experimentally, can constrain the hypotheses about cognition, neural implementation and other stuff of this sort. 

Thanks for stopping by. More soon.
